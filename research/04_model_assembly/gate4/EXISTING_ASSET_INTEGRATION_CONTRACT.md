# Qualified surviving-stock input contract

`research_inputs/asset_survival/lifetime_decisions.json` contains technology candidate values with PENDING status. Acceptance requires an explicit human DecisionReference and a compatible Source; `trace_asset_survival.py --lifetimes` records that file's hash. Source-unit IDs, statuses, years and capacities remain independently verified.

`integration_contract.json` has mapping, performance, resource_groups and InventoryCoverage. Mapping entries are keyed by original bus and contain Node, Country, OriginalPartition, NodePartition, Source, DecisionReference and ApprovalStatus. Source qualification must precede approval; changing the string alone does not bypass numerical/ownership checks.

Performance entries may be keyed by unit or technology. They specify ComponentType, Carrier, Efficiency, FixedOM_EUR_per_MW_e_year, VariableOM_EUR_per_MWh_e, CostBasis=EXISTING_NO_NEW_CAPEX and PerformanceBasis=SOURCE_EXISTING_EQUIPMENT. Profiles specify attribute, source component/type, multiplier and Source; the source network must have identical snapshots. StorageUnit additionally needs MaxHours, EfficiencyStore and CyclicStateOfCharge. Thermal Links need FuelCarrier and PhysicalCO2_t_per_MWh_fuel. Non-fossil supply needs a separate approved dedicated boundary; existing fixed biomass obligations cannot be diverted. Zero fixed OM requires explicit source-backed acceptance, never a blanket default.

Resource groups specify TOTAL_CAPACITY or ADDITIONAL_POTENTIAL, LimitMW and unique NewBuildComponents with capacity-to-MW conversions. Surviving compatible units are deducted once only for total envelopes. All candidate copies share one constraint. Existing-only technologies require explicit evidence of no new shared candidate instead of an invented potential. The builder retains pending units if contracts are incomplete and keeps the overall development qualification until inventory coverage closes.

Use `python tests/research/test_surviving_asset_integration.py` for the synthetic success and rejection paths. This does not approve the fixture's numeric values for research. Future model construction must invoke install_research_constraint_hooks after variables/objective exist; no solver is called by the hook. Single-year interpretation only; group age fields are an envelope of already-screened units, not an averaged-age survival assumption or a multi-year pathway.
