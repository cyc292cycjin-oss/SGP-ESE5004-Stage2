# Final-energy → useful-service construction method

Material Passport: academic-research-suite; method design; Phase 3A-4 v1; 2026-10-01; ANALYZED / ASEAN_VALIDATION_PENDING. **The method is specified; real eleven-country inputs are not ready. No final model input is produced here.**

## Evidence chain and minimum records

`Official final-energy balance → country/region end-use mapping → historical device efficiency/COP → useful space/water service → spatial allocation → temporal allocation → human-reviewed model input`.

Use the [field dictionary](../../../../data_registry/buildings_phase3a4/BUILDINGS_USEFUL_HEAT_INPUT_SCHEMA.csv). It defines the requested fields rather than fabricating country records. [The empty input template](../../../../data_registry/buildings_phase3a4/BUILDINGS_USEFUL_HEAT_INPUT_TEMPLATE.csv) has only column headers and contains zero scientific input rows. Missing is distinct from reported zero; zero needs a source and coverage statement.

1. **Balance basis.** Freeze country/region, R/S classification, source year, publication revision, fuel, energy unit and price fields if relevant. Exclude electricity transformation input from final-consumption totals. Record raw row/table/page, local file/hash and actual source value. Preserve LPG versus aggregated oil where the source permits it; do not assume a single oil heating share. Mass/volume→energy needs explicit calorific value, density/moisture as applicable and HHV/LHV basis. Unit conversion and mapping scripts must be versioned and reproducible.
2. **End uses.** Within each `(country, region, sector, fuel, source year, source-row)` partition, allocate space heating, water heating, cooking and other uses with stated denominators and non-overlapping shares. Cooling electricity remains direct. If the source already gives end-use final energy, do not multiply by a second share; mark `FinalEnergyScope=end_use` and require the mechanically defined identity share 1. That is a scope convention, not an assumed end-use fraction. With fuel-total data, use `FinalEnergyScope=fuel_total`; missing shares stop conversion. An incomplete partition retains `unclassified`, never automatically redistributes it to heat.
3. **Device mix and service.** For fuel-total final energy F and end-use fraction alpha, device-specific final-input fractions beta must sum to 1 within that end use. Compute `Q_space/water = sum_d F_MWh_final * alpha * beta_d * k_d`. `k_d` is historical useful-output/final-input efficiency or seasonal COP at the same boundary. If a source already reports device-specific final energy, do not apply beta twice. For electric HP, historical COP may exceed 1; for boilers/resistance, clarify whether efficiency and fuel energy share the HHV/LHV basis. Never clamp a published LHV efficiency >1 without checking the source definition. Weighted efficiency uses **input-energy weights**, not unweighted device counts. Installed capacities or appliance penetration require utilization evidence before they can become energy weights.
4. **Avoid circularity.** The historical device mix establishes a fixed service baseline. Future model HP/boiler/CHP performance determines optimized input energy to serve Q; it cannot be used to invent the historical service baseline and then described as calibration. Existing DEA/PyPSA efficiencies stay source-tagged and unaccepted as ASEAN calibration until reviewed.
5. **Service boundary.** Choose useful energy at appliance outlet, building delivery, or heat-bus supply explicitly. The recommended accounting contract is delivered useful service, with downstream network/TES losses separate. This remains a boundary decision, not automatic DH adoption. Direct solar thermal/heat commodities require a boundary check so purchased heat and fuel conversion are not both counted for the same service. CHP fuel split cannot be inferred from electricity and heat totals alone; document the allocation convention or use independently measured delivered heat. Do not model cooking supply competition.
6. **Spatial allocation.** Allocate country R/S separately with normalized, nonnegative weights on the actual cluster/country mapping. Region/climate evidence should constrain real heating regions; national shares alone cannot prove every cluster has space heating. Population-only allocation is an unaccepted candidate, especially for S. Preserve the total by country, sector and end use. Do not transfer heat across national borders through the allocation step. No European stock or default-zero ASEAN stock may supply missing weights.
7. **Temporal allocation.** For annual useful energy Q_i and shape phi_it, set `q_it = Q_i * phi_it / sum_t(w_t*phi_it)` in MW_th. Record time zone, leap year, weather year, source year, represented-period weights and resampling order. If Q>0 and the weighted shape integral is zero, NaN, negative or non-finite, stop with the node/account ID. Q=0 is allowed only when its source status supports zero; a missing annual value cannot become zero through fillna. E2 protects profiles but does not fix the earlier annual-table fillna path.
8. **Historical electric profile.** Allocate D/E using appropriate electric-input profiles matched to A* and H/I coverage. An annual COP or heating share does not identify their hourly shape. Validate `A*-D-E>=0` pointwise after any transfer to B/C. Do not smooth, clip or cap heating until it fits the base profile without an accepted method.
9. **Review and export.** Require the [E3 accounting identities](BUILDINGS_E3_ACCOUNTING_SPEC.md), source lineage, documented transformations and human decisions before export. Export a manifest including source hashes, config identities, method version, input/output units, software versions, target year and reviewed boundary. This phase exports no ready-to-solve dataset.

## Temporal choices allowed for future review

**Water heating:** an evidenced usage-based daily profile, with R/S weekday/weekend/seasonal differences where observed. Water-use litres and timing are not useful heat without the hot-water fraction, inlet/delivery temperatures, and boundary/loss definition. The retained Hanoi experiment is a small, short overnight high-income household study, not an eleven-country or Services profile. It does not justify manufacturing hourly arrays.

**Space heating:** require actual region-specific space-heating service evidence first; then test an evidenced temperature-response/usage shape. Zero HDD with positive annual service is an inconsistency to investigate, not a zero demand. The absence of a source column is not observed absence of heating. BDEW is a reproducibility/reference benchmark only. No uniform ASEAN 60/40 split is permitted.

## Executability

The method is algorithmically specified and the **synthetic identity contract** is executable in `check_accounting_contract.py`. It is not an E3 implementation and cannot write network inputs. Real final-energy→service execution is blocked on the fields in the data-requirements table: accepted end-use shares, historical device/input-energy mix, efficiencies/COP and boundary/year reconciliation, followed by spatial/temporal mapping. Missing fields remain blank; no current config value is copied into the scientific input template.
