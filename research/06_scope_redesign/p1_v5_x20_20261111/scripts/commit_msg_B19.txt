research.demand.v5.distribution_grid: lv_pypsa_eur (v5 main) | hv_lossless
(v4). v5_layers.apply_distribution_grid_v5 (PyPSA-Eur v2025.07.0
insert_electricity_distribution_grid): every Load with 'electric' in its
carrier on an AC node (electricity, industry electricity, agriculture
machinery electric) and bus0 of BEV chargers (smart / uncontrolled),
industrial heat pumps, industrial electric boilers and electric cooking
(Q3 (b)) move to the node's low-voltage bus; electrolysis, DAC, Haber-Bosch
and grid batteries stay on HV. The distribution grid link becomes forward
(p_min_pu 0, efficiency 0.97, PyPSA-Eur config L762-763) plus a '-reversed'
twin (LV -> HV, 0.97, capital cost 0, length 0, no FOM); the existing hook
research_lossy_bidirectional_sync collects every extendable reversed link,
so the twins are synchronised without a new list. Loads are not scaled
(Q4 (a)). The demand ledger rows (S2_DEMAND_OWNERSHIP) follow the moved
loads; LV buses without location get their node. An AC node with a moved
load but no LV bus stops the build.

s2_network calls the layer after the transmission / AC loss layers (they
refuse existing reversed links and zero lengths), before the dedicated
lines and the cost scaling. Toy-network tests: 56 passed.
