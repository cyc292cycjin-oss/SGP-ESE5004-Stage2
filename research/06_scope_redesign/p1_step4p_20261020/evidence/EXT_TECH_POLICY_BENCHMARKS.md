**Audit reading (A):** Only VN (binding decision), PH (plan), ID (decrees) and TH (draft PDP) carry numeric targets; the sum of all stated 2050-horizon targets is roughly VN 10.5-14 GW + PH 4.8 GW + TH 9 GW + ID (500 MW 2032-33, 44 GW by *2060*) + MM 0.11-0.33 GW. MY, SG, LA, KH, BN, TL have no capacity target and SG/KH explicitly have no deployment decision. A model that allows *unlimited* SMR in all 11 countries (incl. SG) and unlimited large PWR in 10 is therefore not supported by current policy; at minimum SG, KH, BN, TL, LA, MM large-PWR should be constrained and SMR capped by a policy scenario.
