## Summary: model values vs benchmarks

1. **Green H2 import 105.8 EUR2020/MWh (~3.5 EUR/kg)** - HIGH vs IRENA 2050 delivered 1.5-2 USD/kg (Japan landed ammonia ~1.0 + 0.4 cracking; suppliers 1.25-1.45) and vs Hydrogen Council 2050 production 1-2 USD/kg + IRENA transport 0.7-1.6; slightly ABOVE Neumann ship-H2 84 EUR2020/MWh and derivatives 88-110 EUR2020/MWh (by construction, x1.2); WITHIN BNEF 2050 production 1.60-5.09 USD/kg plus transport. Overall: upper-middle of the literature, conservative rather than optimistic.
2. **SMR 8,421 EUR2020/kW (= AEO8 9,600 kUSD/MW)** - WITHIN EIA/S&L 2024 (8,936 USD2023/kW) and Lazard large-nuclear 9,020-14,820 USD/kW; HIGH vs INL/ATB 2050 moderate 4,000 and advanced 2,000 USD2022/kW, close to conservative 6,250; far BELOW NuScale CFPP actual 20,139 USD/kW; well ABOVE KHNP i-SMR target 3,500 USD/kWe. Keeping 2050 capex flat at 2024-level is conservative relative to ATB learning assumptions.
3. **SMR FOM 1.15 %/a (~110 USD/kW-yr), VOM 1.93 EUR/MWh** - LOW end vs INL 118-216 USD/kW-yr and EIA 122; VOM below INL 2.2-2.8 and EIA 3.19. Lifetime 40 a matches technology-data.
4. **Large nuclear** - WEO 2025 China/India 2,500-2,800 USD2024/kW in 2050 (nth-of-a-kind) vs technology-data v0.15.0 10,805.7 EUR2025/kW (Lazard/Vogtle-based; note: not 7,000-8,500 as assumed in the brief). Asia benchmarks are 3-4x below the European/US-based datasets.
5. **Offshore wind 2050** - technology-data 1,916 EUR2025/kW; WEO 2025 China 880, India 1,460, EU 1,980 USD2024/kW; IRENA Asia 2024 actual 2,467 USD2024/kW (China 1,520). World Bank Viet Nam LCOE 51-62 USD/MWh by 2035. No quantified typhoon capex premium found (only +34 EUR/MWh LCOE effect, China, 2012 study).
6. **Solar/onshore 2050** - technology-data 368 / 1,286 EUR2025/kW vs WEO 2025 India 250 / 1,010, China 280 / 790 USD2024/kW: technology-data is HIGH for Asia on PV and onshore.
7. **LNG 2050** - WEO 2025 Japan STEPS 9.7, NZE 4.9 USD2024/MBtu confirmed (Table 2.3, before regasification); regas adders observed 0.3-1.3 USD/MMBtu (Japan ~0.5).
